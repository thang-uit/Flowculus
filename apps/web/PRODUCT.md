# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People learning and reviewing business process modelling, cycle-time analysis and
process mining, including the project's university coursework context.

## Product Purpose

Flowculus connects an editable BPMN subset to explainable calculations and event-log
discovery. Its knowledge library helps readers find formulas, their assumptions and
worked examples without leaving the product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, React Flow and pure domain packages.
- Browser-only models, local drafts, event logs and worker computation; no backend.
- Existing modelling, mining, import/export and semantic confirmation stay available.
- Each numerical result states its quality; unsupported semantics withhold a number.
- Knowledge content describes the supported implementation and relevant theory,
  including the distinction between them. It never changes the active workspace.
- English and Vietnamese, keyboard access, small screens, light/dark themes and
  reduced motion are required.

## Brand Commitments

Flowculus, its existing yellow identity and Plus Jakarta Sans remain the visual
authority. The user selected the DataFusion reading-guide website as a reference for
search, contents navigation, illustrated explanations and readable documentation.

## Product Principles

- Explain the assumptions alongside each formula.
- Help users distinguish observations from normative process violations.
- Make real diagrams and demonstrations serve understanding.
- Preserve the user's current model while they consult the documentation.
